# Security

## Data Boundary

No secrets, private Trust records, beneficiary data, banking data, tax data, credential data, address data, or identity records belong in this repository.

## Environment Handling

Environment examples must remain placeholder-only. Real .env files, local overrides, and machine-specific secrets must stay untracked.

## Source Asset Preservation

The source logo and canon packet under docs/source are preservation records. They must remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Any later derivative assets must be non-destructive and traceable.

## Non-Destructive Logo Rules

Production derivatives may crop, mask, or recompose presentation surfaces only when the original source remains archived intact and the legal footer or other inaccurate text is not destructively overwritten.

## Dependency Review

Later dependencies must be reviewed for purpose, cost, data access, and maintenance risk before approval. No dependency is authorized in Build Room 01.

## External Link Safety

External URLs must be verified before use, opened with safe link attributes, and distinguished from internal routes. Placeholder links are not acceptable for public claims.

## Legal Classification Controls

Content must honor the registered assumed-name, program, media, mission, and Trust privacy boundaries locked by the governing canon.

## Deployment and DNS Gates

Deployment provider selection, production publishing, and DNS changes require separate founder approval and are not authorized in Build Room 01.
