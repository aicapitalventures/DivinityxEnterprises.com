## 1. Founder Visual Lock

The Divinityx Enterprises website must share the same visual world, background character, emotional tone, and premium design language as the founder-approved Divinityx Enterprises logo image.

The image is not merely a logo file. It is the governing visual reference for:

- the site background atmosphere;
- color relationships;
- lighting;
- depth;
- metallic-gold treatment;
- celestial and technological detail;
- typography character;
- spacing and ceremony;
- perceived authority;
- and the balance between faith, intelligence, enterprise, and legacy.

Future interface work must be evaluated against this reference before founder approval.

---

## 2. Required Emotional and Brand Effect

The website must feel:

- premium and established;
- institutional rather than promotional;
- celestial but controlled;
- spiritually resonant without resembling a church flyer;
- technologically intelligent without resembling a cryptocurrency or gaming site;
- powerful without becoming aggressive;
- ceremonial without becoming ornamental clutter;
- dark, luminous, spacious, and intentional;
- worthy of a long-term corporate and generational institution.

The design should create the impression that Divinityx Enterprises is the central governing house of a serious ecosystem.

---

## 3. Background System Lock

Every primary public route must live within the same cosmic visual environment as the reference asset.

Required background characteristics:

1. A dominant cosmic-black and midnight-navy base.
2. Deep blue nebular dimension concentrated selectively rather than spread uniformly.
3. Fine gold stardust and warm-gold star points.
4. Sparse celestial-blue points that add depth without changing the navy-and-gold identity.
5. Occasional controlled star flares near major focal points.
6. Very faint gold circuitry, constellation, grid, or linear details where they reinforce the relationship between legacy and intelligence.
7. Dark negative space around content so the site remains readable and authoritative.
8. Seamless transitions between sections; no abrupt white or unrelated background blocks.

The full portrait reference image must not be tiled, stretched, or used carelessly as a repeating wallpaper. A later approved asset room may create a background-only derivative from the original image while preserving its star field, nebular depth, gold particles, and dark tonal structure. The original source must remain unchanged.

## 5. Light and Material Language

Required visual treatment:

- polished metallic gold with champagne highlights and bronze shadow depth;
- narrow luminous edges rather than thick neon outlines;
- soft radiance around the corporate crest and key calls to action;
- controlled black and navy shadows that make gold feel physical;
- subtle glass or translucent navy panels only when content legibility benefits;
- hairline gold borders and restrained inner highlights;
- sparse flares and particles used as hierarchy, not decoration everywhere.

Forbidden treatment:

- flat mustard yellow standing in for gold;
- uncontrolled glow on every card;
- bright-blue neon dominance;
- rainbow gradients;
- excessive glassmorphism;
- heavy drop shadows that muddy the background;
- noisy particle animation behind body copy.

---

## 6. Corporate Crest and Logo Hierarchy

1. The Divinityx Enterprises crest and wordmark remain the dominant identity.
2. The original 941 × 1672 source image is preserved unchanged in the project source archive.
3. Production derivatives may include a transparent crest, compact emblem, horizontal wordmark, hero composition, favicon, social preview, and background-only texture.
4. Every derivative must be non-destructive and traceable to the source asset.
5. The crest may never be stretched, recolored, skewed, clipped unintentionally, or placed on a background that weakens its gold and navy contrast.
6. Ecosystem logos must remain subordinate to the parent crest and appear inside governed directory cards rather than forming an unstructured logo collage.
7. Division accent colors remain localized and may not replace the corporate cosmic navy-and-gold environment.

---

## 7. Typography Character

The reference establishes two complementary typographic voices:

- a classical, high-authority Roman or inscriptional serif for major headings and the corporate name;
- a disciplined, letter-spaced supporting face for labels, navigation, classifications, and metadata.

Requirements:

- Major headings should feel carved, formal, and enduring.
- Labels may use uppercase lettering with controlled tracking.
- Body copy must use a highly readable web face and must not imitate decorative logo lettering.
- Typography must remain calm and spacious; do not compress content into dense blocks.
- Script or ornamental type may appear only in tiny decorative details, never navigation or body copy.

Exact web fonts and licensing remain a later Build Room decision. No external font service is authorized by this canon.

---

## 8. Interface Surfaces and Components

### Header

- Near-black or transparent midnight surface integrated into the cosmic background.
- Compact crest or approved wordmark derivative.
- Fine gold divider or border, visible focus states, restrained glow.
- No row of ecosystem logos.

### Hero

- Corporate crest is the visual anchor.
- Deep navy nebular concentration and gold starlight should focus attention toward the crest and headline.
- Copy and calls to action must remain readable without covering important logo details.
- The final layout may use an approved crest/wordmark derivative rather than forcing the full portrait source into an unsuitable desktop crop.

### Ecosystem cards

- Dark navy/black surfaces with dimensional depth.
- Fine gold boundary treatment and localized accent color.
- Subtle illumination on hover and strong visible focus.
- No uncontrolled logo enlargement, spinning, or continuous flashing.

### Buttons

- Primary: luminous or metallic gold surface with dark readable text.
- Secondary: transparent/dark surface with a gold border and warm-ivory text.
- Hover and focus states may brighten but must not bloom excessively.

### Footer

- Return to deep cosmic black.
- Restrained gold rule, accurate legal disclosure, warm-ivory text.
- The ending should feel conclusive and institutional.

---

## 9. Motion Law

Permitted motion:

- very slow atmospheric drift;
- subtle opacity shifts in star fields;
- restrained card lift or border illumination;
- elegant section reveals;
- rare focal shimmer on a major gold element.

Motion must be optional, low-cost, and disabled or simplified when `prefers-reduced-motion` is active.

Forbidden motion:

- autoplay video;
- rapid twinkling;
- flashing star fields;
- constant logo pulsing;
- heavy parallax;
- animation libraries added only for spectacle.

---

## 10. Accessibility and Readability Locks

- Decorative stars, circuitry, and nebula layers must not reduce text contrast.
- Long-form copy should use warm ivory or another verified high-contrast foreground—not dim bronze or muted gold.
- Background artwork must be decorative to assistive technology unless it conveys necessary meaning.
- The corporate name and navigation cannot exist only inside the logo image.
- Focus indicators must remain visible against both black and gold surfaces.
- Mobile layouts must preserve the crest without accidental cropping.
- Star density and effects should be reduced where smaller screens would become visually noisy.
- All interface contrast must be tested before release.

---

## 11. Forbidden Visual Drift

The site must not become:

- a generic black-and-gold template;
- a flat white corporate brochure;
- a bright-blue technology startup page;
- a fantasy, astrology, or gaming interface;
- a cryptocurrency or investment-fund landing page;
- a church flyer;
- a wall of competing logos;
- a particle-effects demonstration;
- or a visual system in which the operating divisions overpower the Divinityx Enterprises identity.

---

## 12. Later Asset-Room Requirements

Before the visual system is implemented, the authorized asset Build Room must:

1. preserve the uploaded source image byte-for-byte in the archive;
2. record its filename, dimensions, format, and SHA-256;
3. create only approved non-destructive derivatives;
4. create a responsive background-only treatment that retains the reference image's exact visual world;
5. optimize production files without overwriting the source;
6. verify transparent edges and logo legibility;
7. test desktop, tablet, and mobile compositions;
8. document every derivative in the asset manifest;
9. provide founder-review screenshots before release;
10. stop if a derivative materially changes the crest, wordmark, palette, or brand impression.

---

## 13. Visual Verification Gate

The visual release candidate passes only when:

- the website is immediately recognizable as belonging to the uploaded Divinityx Enterprises logo;
- the cosmic background atmosphere persists across every primary route;
- the parent crest is dominant;
- gold appears metallic and luminous rather than yellow and flat;
- navy and black preserve depth without crushing readability;
- blue remains a secondary celestial accent;
- cards and sections feel integrated into one visual world;
- mobile presentation preserves hierarchy and logo integrity;
- accessibility and reduced-motion checks pass;
- and Elijah Lee Cooley approves desktop and mobile screenshot evidence.
