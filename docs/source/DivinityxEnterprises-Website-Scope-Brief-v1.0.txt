PROJECT PURPOSE

Build the official corporate website and digital headquarters for Divinityx Enterprises LLC.

This is not intended to be a large customer-service platform, e-commerce website, investor portal, or separate operating-company website.

Its primary functions are to:

1. establish Divinityx Enterprises LLC as a legitimate parent and operating company;
2. explain the purpose and structure of the Divinityx ecosystem;
3. distinguish registered DBAs from brands, programs, systems, media properties, and developing initiatives;
4. display approved ecosystem logos as premium clickable directory cards;
5. route visitors to the correct external website or internal overview page;
6. provide a professional corporate contact destination;
7. preserve legal accuracy without publishing private Trust or family-governance information.

The site should feel like the official headquarters of a serious, long-term business ecosystem.
